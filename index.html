About Ribosomes Diagnostics

Our Story

Established on 21st February 2020 in Panvel, Maharashtra, Ribosomes Diagnostics has emerged as one of the most trusted and reputed marketers & distributors of diagnostic products in India.

We specialize in providing cutting-edge diagnostic equipment including Hematology Analyzers, Biochemistry Analyzers, CLIA Systems, ELISA Readers, and Coagulation Analyzers.

Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction has made us a preferred partner for healthcare facilities across the nation.

To provide world-class diagnostic solutions that enhance healthcare delivery across India through innovation and excellence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What products do you offer?

We offer a comprehensive range of diagnostic equipment including Hematology Analyzers (3-part, 5-part, 6-part), Biochemistry Analyzers (fully automated and semi-automated), CLIA Systems, ELISA Readers, Coagulation Analyzers, and Electrolyte Analyzers.

Do you provide installation and training?

Yes! We provide professional installation by certified technicians and comprehensive training for your staff. Our training includes hands-on sessions, operation manuals, and video tutorials.

What is your warranty policy?

All our products come with manufacturer warranty. The warranty period varies by product (typically 1-2 years). We also offer extended warranty and Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for continued support.

Do you offer 24/7 support?

Yes! We provide 24/7 technical support through phone, email, and our AI chatbot. For emergencies, our technicians are available round-the-clock to ensure minimal downtime.

How can I get a quote?

You can request a quote by calling us at +91 773 889 5976, filling out our contact form, or chatting with our AI assistant. We'll provide a detailed quotation based on your specific requirements.

What are your delivery timelines?

Delivery timelines vary by product and location. Typically, we deliver within 7-15 business days for in-stock items. Custom orders may take 4-6 weeks. We'll provide exact timelines with your quote.

Do you provide spare parts?

Yes, we maintain a comprehensive inventory of spare parts for all products we distribute. Spare parts are available on demand with quick delivery to minimize equipment downtime.

Can I see a demo before purchasing?

Absolutely! We offer product demonstrations at our facility or can arrange on-site demos at your location. Contact us to schedule a demo at your convenience.
