About Ribosomes Diagnostics

Our Story

Established on 21st February 2020 in Panvel, Maharashtra, Ribosomes Diagnostics has emerged as one of the most trusted and reputed marketers & distributors of diagnostic products in India.

We specialize in providing cutting-edge diagnostic equipment including Hematology Analyzers, Biochemistry Analyzers, CLIA Systems, ELISA Readers, and Coagulation Analyzers.

Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction has made us a preferred partner for healthcare facilities across the nation.
